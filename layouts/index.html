The goal of the day

A credible benchmark cannot only check whether an AI Scientist reaches the right answer: it must evaluate how the result was produced. By the end of the day we want first shared answers, in the form of:

  • A v0 shared specification: task card, trace format, metric primitives and baselines, contamination protocol, judge policy, evaluating explanations.
  • One-page summaries from Track A and Track B.
  • An outline of a community whitepaper, with co-authors from the room.

Lightning talks have no Q&A on stage. Questions and answers go to each talk's Miro board and to the working streams; participants received the links by e-mail.
